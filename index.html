Are T Levels recognized by top employers in the North West? +

Yes. The qualification structure was explicitly co-developed by industry giants including ARM, BBC, and Cisco. Insights captured within the Employer "survey 2022" indicate that 90% of participating companies view T Level placements as an "extended interview process" to secure incoming talent ahead of competitors.

What financial and equipment support does Ada Manchester guarantee? +

We completely eliminate material barriers to entry. Every student enrolled at Ada Manchester receives a free state-of-the-art laptop for the duration of their studies and direct financial travel support to cover commuting costs to the Ancoats campus and workplace placements.

Can a student with SEND or from a lower socio-economic background thrive here? +

Dismantling compound disadvantage is our primary mission. While national data confirms that individuals facing overlapping socio-economic barriers are statistically three times more likely to fall out of education or work, Ada actively subverts this. 50% of our student community comes from low-income households, and we provide every learner with dedicated one-to-one coaching from active tech professionals alongside elite pastoral care systems.

What are the absolute entry conditions for Ada Manchester? +

Applicants require a GCSE average grade score of 5, which must explicitly include Grade 5 or above in both English and Mathematics. Eligible candidates then attend a specialized problem-solving assessment event on campus.
